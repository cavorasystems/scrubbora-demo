Our densest routes. Same-week starts are usually available here.

Gordon Head · Cordova Bay · Royal Oak · Broadmead · Quadra Village

Oak Bay→

Character homes built before 1940, plus Uplands estates that need two cleaners and a lighter hand on original surfaces.

Uplands · Willows · Gonzales · Estevan · South Oak Bay

Esquimalt→

Naval community with older homes and a growing number of condos. Frequent postings mean steady move-in and move-out work.

Esquimalt Village · Saxe Point · Rockheights

View Royal→

Newer subdivisions and townhomes between the core and the Westshore. Easy parking, straightforward recurring cleans.

Thetis Lake · Helmcken · Portage Inlet

The Westshore

No travel minimum

The fastest-growing part of the region, and where most new-build cleaning is.

Langford→

The fastest-growing city in BC. New builds, townhome complexes and young families — heavy demand for recurring bi-weekly work.

Westhills · Bear Mountain · Happy Valley · Goldstream

Colwood→

New subdivisions and ocean-view homes. Post-construction cleaning is a steady line of work here.

Royal Bay · Latoria · Triangle Mountain · Colwood Corners

Highlands→

Rural acreage and larger custom homes. Usually a two-cleaner visit; we schedule these together to keep the route sensible.

Millstream · Caleb Pike

Metchosin→

Rural properties and farmhouses. Best booked on the same day as Colwood or Langford work.

Happy Valley · William Head · Pedder Bay

The Saanich Peninsula

No travel minimum

Sidney has the oldest average age in the region, and our seniors service is busiest here.

Sidney→

Condos, townhomes and a large retired population. Weekly seniors slots with the same cleaner are our most requested service in Sidney.

Beacon Avenue · Sidney by the Sea · Resthaven

North Saanich→

Larger properties on acreage near the airport and ferry terminal. Mostly weekly and bi-weekly recurring work.

Deep Cove · Ardmore · Lands End

Central Saanich→

Farmland, village homes and newer developments. Brentwood Bay and Saanichton are on our regular peninsula route.

Brentwood Bay · Saanichton · Turgoose

Sooke & the west

Sooke→

About 45 minutes from the core, so we run a set day each week. Vacation homes, move-outs and larger recurring jobs.

Sooke Village · Whiffin Spit · East Sooke · Otter Point

Not sure if you're in range? If you're inside the Capital Regional District, you almost certainly are. Our business licence is the Greater Victoria Inter-Municipal Licence, which covers all thirteen municipalities, so there's no jurisdiction we can't legally work in.

Commercial and strata work is region-wide too. Dental and medical offices, strata common areas and small commercial spaces are cleaned on the same footprint, evenings, on a set schedule.
