Questions

Straight answers

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, everything is included in the price. If you'd rather we use specific products in your home, leave them out and tell us.

Do I really get the same cleaner every time?

Yes. It's the main thing we're built around. If your regular cleaner is away, we tell you in advance rather than sending a stranger unannounced.

Are your cleaners employees or subcontractors?

Employees. They're trained to our method, insured under our policy and covered by WorkSafeBC. Subcontracting is cheaper to run and worse for you.

What if something isn't right?

Tell us within 24 hours and we come back and fix it at no charge. No argument.

Do you clean dental and medical offices?

Yes, after hours, with an infection-control checklist, colour-coded microfibre, documented disinfectant dwell times and a photo log after every visit.

What areas do you cover?

All of Greater Victoria: Victoria, Saanich, Oak Bay, Esquimalt, View Royal, Colwood, Langford, Highlands, Metchosin, Sooke, Central Saanich, North Saanich and Sidney.

Is there anything you don't do?

We don't do laundry, dishes, pet waste, personal errands, or anything needing a ladder above two steps. Keeping that line clear is how we keep the price honest.
